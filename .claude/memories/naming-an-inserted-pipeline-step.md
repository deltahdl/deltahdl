# Naming an inserted pipeline step

Give an inserted pipeline step a real position or a descriptive name, never "Step 0".

**Why:** "Step 0" names the step by the fact that it was added later rather than by what it does, and it only stays coherent until something else has to go in front of it. Nothing enforces the choice.

**How to apply:** Renumber the steps so the new one has a real position, or drop the numbering for that step and name it after its work.

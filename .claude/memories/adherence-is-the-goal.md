# Adherence is the goal

deltahdl exists to adhere to IEEE 1800-2023 in full, and to show that it does. A release is not the aim, and neither is a date. A plan, a review of the process or a ranking of the open work is judged by one question: does it bring deltahdl closer to full adherence, or make that adherence easier to show?

**Why:** A departure from the standard was there before anyone found it, and so was a gap in what the tests cover. Finding one is the work succeeding. Advice that treats the growing list as the fault, and answers it with a finish line the standard does not set or with fewer findings filed, gives up the accuracy the project is for.

**How to apply:**

- When passes over the standard keep finding departures or coverage gaps, ask why the earlier passes missed them and how the next one can be shown complete. Never ask how to stop the list growing.
- Never propose reaching an end sooner through a finish line set by anything other than the standard, or through filing fewer findings.
- Weigh a change to the process, the CI or the code by what it does for accuracy, and say so in those terms.
- Read the clause before saying what it requires, per [lrm-source-of-truth](lrm-source-of-truth.md).

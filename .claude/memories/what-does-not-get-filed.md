# What does not get filed

Two findings do not become issues.

A defect the commit in hand fixes. The commit message states it and the issue would close on the same push.

A defect an open issue already covers. Cite that issue instead.

**Why:** A second issue over one defect gives one piece of work two entries, and closing either leaves the other claiming there is something left.

**How to apply:** Before filing anything — a finding of research per [research-lives-in-issues](research-lives-in-issues.md), or one the user asked to have tracked — ask whether the change in hand already closes it, and search the tracker for an existing one. A problem met while working is not filed at all; it is solved, per [solving-what-a-session-finds](solving-what-a-session-finds.md).
